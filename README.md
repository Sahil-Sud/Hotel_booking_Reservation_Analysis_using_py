# Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis: Enhancing Revenue and Efficiency

## Introduction  
This project analyzes the high cancellation rates at **City Hotel** and **Resort Hotel**, with the aim of identifying key factors and providing actionable recommendations. The goal is to reduce cancellations, boost revenue, and enhance overall operational efficiency.

## Objective  
The primary objective of this analysis is to understand the factors contributing to high cancellation rates in both hotels. By uncovering these factors, we can develop strategies to minimize cancellations, increase revenue, and optimize room usage.

## Methodology  
In this project, we analyze hotel booking cancellations by exploring various factors, including:
- **Pricing Strategies**
- **Seasonality**
- **Hotel Location**
- **Service Quality**

The goal is to identify what drives cancellations and where improvements can be made.

## Key Findings  
- Identified key factors influencing cancellations at **City Hotel** and **Resort Hotel**  
- Evaluated the impact of **pricing strategies** and **seasonal variations** on cancellation rates  
- Assessed the correlation between **service quality** and cancellation trends  
- Developed strategies to reduce cancellations and increase revenue  
- Suggested ways to optimize room utilization and improve operational efficiency

## Conclusion  
By understanding the factors behind hotel booking cancellations, this project provides insights that can help **City Hotel** and **Resort Hotel** reduce cancellations, optimize room usage, and enhance their revenue strategies. Implementing these recommendations will drive customer satisfaction and foster long-term success in the competitive hospitality industry.
